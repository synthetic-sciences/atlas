# Code of Conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss the work, explain your
reasoning, and make room for different experience levels and perspectives.

We expect participants to:

- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept corrections in good faith.
- Respect others' time, boundaries, identity, and privacy.
- Keep private research, personal information, and credentials out of shared
  examples, issues, and conversations.
- Credit other people's contributions and preserve their license terms.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized behavior, sustained personal
attacks, and publishing someone else's private information are unacceptable.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other
project spaces, including when representing Atlas elsewhere. Maintainers may
remove content or restrict participation to protect the community. They should
explain their decisions and handle reports with care for the people involved.

Report conduct concerns privately to **support@sayntheticsciences.ai**. Include
enough context to understand what happened without sharing unrelated private
information. Security reports belong at the channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
